AMAZON CUSTOMER ACCOUNT & SERVICES

EPIC  →  FEATURES  →  USER STORIES  →  TASKS

Project Name: Amazon Customer Account Hub

Reference: Customer account dashboard screenshot supplied for this exercise.

Project Overview

This project organises the customer account dashboard into seven Azure DevOps epics. Each epic represents one visible area in the account page: Your Orders, Returns and Refunds, Manage Addresses, Manage Prime, Payment Settings, Account Settings, and Digital Services and Device Support. The backlog describes the customer-facing functions, the expected behaviour, and the development and testing work needed to deliver each area.

Work Item Structure

Epic – a major customer account capability shown on the account dashboard.

Feature – a functional group within an epic that can be planned and delivered.

User Story – a customer or support-user outcome with acceptance criteria.

Task – a specific design, development, integration, data, or testing activity under a user story.

Planning note: Estimates and priorities are practice values for Azure DevOps planning. Actual integrations, policies, membership terms, payment providers and device capabilities must be confirmed before implementation.

EPIC 1 – YOUR ORDERS

Work Item Type: Epic

Parent: None

Priority: 1

Risk: High

Description: Provide customers with one place to review purchases, check shipment progress, view order details, and take permitted actions such as cancelling an order. The area keeps order information consistent across the account page, order detail page, shipment updates and customer notifications.

Business Value: Customers can find order information without contacting support, understand delivery progress, and resolve eligible order issues quickly.

Success Criteria:

Customers can see current and past orders with accurate status and order totals.

Shipment milestones and estimated delivery information are refreshed from the fulfilment source.

Eligible orders can be cancelled and the result is reflected in the order history.

Order details are visible only to the signed-in customer and authorised support staff.
